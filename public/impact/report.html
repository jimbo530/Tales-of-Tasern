How It Works

CHAR tokens are biochar carbon removal credits certified by Puro.earth and tokenized by Toucan Protocol. Each CHAR = 1 tonne CO2 removed. Every MycoPad launch allocates 6% of its seed to a CHAR reactor that burns credits permanently every 2 hours.

How the Reactor Network Creates Impact

Every swap through a reactor pool generates 1% fees. Every 2 hours, reactors collect fees, burn tokens permanently, and deepen liquidity. 6% of every MycoPad launch seed funds a CHAR carbon reactor. The network runs autonomously — no one can stop or withdraw from it.

All reactors are permissionless (anyone can call execute()) and 5 are fully renounced (immutable, no admin).
