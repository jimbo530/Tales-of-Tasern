Burn Mechanics

  • Every swap through a POOP V3 pool generates 1% fees
  • Fees are collected and burned every 2.4 hours — POOP side burned directly, other side sold for blue chips or locked
  • 11 pools burning simultaneously: WETH, USDC, cbBTC + 8 community tokens
  • Burns and LP additions compound every 2.4hr keeper cycle — deeper liquidity each round
  • Mint rate is fixed by gameplay — baselings produce POOP based on locked LP value
  • Mint rate does not scale with demand — burns do
  • All LP permanently locked — no withdrawals, ever
  • Dead and frozen game assets become pure burn engines
  • External market volatility (ETH/BTC price moves) generates burns passively

Transparency

  • Mint access: only addresses with isMinter role can mint. Currently only the game contract (BaselingNFT) has minter access.
  • 24hr timelock: all minter role changes must be proposed on-chain and wait 24 hours before execution.
  • No max supply: emissions are controlled by LP fee revenue, not a hard cap.
  • Phantom accounting: when game assets die or freeze, LP fees are still generated but never enter circulation. totalSupply() is always the accurate circulating figure.
  • No pause or freeze: transfers cannot be blocked by anyone.
  • 130 lines of Solidity, no proxy, no inheritance: read it yourself on Basescan
